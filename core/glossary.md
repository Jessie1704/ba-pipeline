# Organisation Glossary

Shared business vocabulary. Feature-level terms live in each feature's `04-model.md` and are
promoted here when they are used by more than one feature.

Rules: one meaning per term; list synonyms people actually use; note terms that must **not** be used.
Changes to this file are recorded in `audit/workspace.jsonl`.

| Term | Definition | Synonyms (accepted) | Don't say | Owner | Source |
|------|------------|---------------------|-----------|-------|--------|
| _Example:_ Customer | A person or company with at least one completed order | Client | User (ambiguous: means system login) | Sales Ops | D-001 |
