# Kickoff meeting — Expense claims (PILOT — fictional data)

Date: 2026-10-20 · Attendees: Linh Tran (Finance Controller), Marco Rossi (HR Operations Lead),
Aisha Khan (IT — ERP owner), pilot BA

Raw notes as taken:

- Today: employees fill the Excel template, email it to their manager with scanned/photographed receipts.
  Manager replies "approved". Finance re-keys everything into the ERP. Paid via payroll.
- ~400 employees, roughly 900 claims a month, big peak in the last week of the month.
- Linh: Finance spends about 3 days every month re-keying. Claims get lost in email. Average time from
  submission to reimbursement is 19 days. About 6% of claims come back to the employee because something is missing.
- Linh: "Claims over €500 need my approval too."
- Marco: "And anything over €1,000 should go to the department head."
- Want a web portal: employee submits with receipt photo, manager approves, finance checks and it goes
  into the ERP for payment in the payroll run.
- Most people submit from laptops; field staff (Marco: ~120 people) would love to do it from their phone.
- Aisha: ERP can import a CSV of journal lines once a day. There is an API but we don't have the licence.
- Receipts needed over €25.
- Marco: "People must submit within 60 days, otherwise it's too late."
- Target: live by end of Q1 2027.
