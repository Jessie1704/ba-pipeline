# Expense Policy v3.2 (excerpt) — effective 2024-01-01 (PILOT — fictional data)

4.1 Expenses must be claimed within **90 days** of the date the expense was incurred.
4.2 An itemised receipt is required for every expense above €25. Card slips are not itemised receipts.
5.1 Every claim requires approval by the employee's line manager.
5.2 Claims with a total above €500 additionally require approval by the Finance Controller.
5.3 No employee may approve their own claim or a claim they are named on.
5.4 When a manager is absent for more than 3 working days, their nominated delegate may approve.
6.1 Personal expenses, fines and alcohol are never reimbursed.
6.2 Leavers' outstanding approved claims are paid in their final salary.
